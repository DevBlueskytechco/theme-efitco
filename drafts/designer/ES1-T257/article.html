How to stretch properly after training

Nov 5, 2026By Efitco Team2 min read

A short cool-down helps your muscles relax and keeps you moving well for the next session. Five to ten minutes is enough when you stretch the muscles you just worked.

When to stretch

Stretch right after your last set, while your muscles are still warm. Breathe slowly and stop at mild tension, never at pain.

Hold each stretch for 30 seconds

  • Hamstrings: sit with one leg straight and reach for your toes.
  • Hip flexors: kneel on a training mat and push your hips forward.
  • Chest and shoulders: place a forearm on a rack upright and turn away.
Consistency beats intensity. Stretch a little after every session.

Add a recovery roller on rest days to work through tight spots in your calves and upper back.
