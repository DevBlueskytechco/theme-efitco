Questions and Answers

Quick Answers Before You Write

How long does shipping take?

Orders ship within one business day. Standard delivery is 2-4 business days across the continental US, and shipping is free on orders over $99.

Are adjustable dumbbells easy for beginners?

Yes. The dial changes the weight in seconds, from 11 to 66 lb per hand, so you can start light and add load as you get stronger. There are no plates to swap.

How much floor space do I actually need?

A pair of adjustable dumbbells takes just 0.18 m² and fits under a desk. A complete corner setup fits in 2.4 x 2.0 m and folds flat when you're done.

Does equipment arrive assembled?

Adjustable dumbbells arrive ready to use, with no assembly needed. Other equipment varies, so check the product page before you order.

What's covered by the warranty?

Every item comes with a 12-month warranty, and you can return it within 90 days for your money back. Contact us with your order number and we'll help from there.
