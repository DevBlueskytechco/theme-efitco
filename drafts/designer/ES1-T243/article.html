How to stretch properly after training

Nov 5, 2026·2 min read

On This Page

  1. Why stretch after training
  2. Static or dynamic?
  3. A 10-minute cool-down routine
  4. Common mistakes

Five to ten minutes of stretching after a workout helps your muscles cool down and keeps your range of motion where it should be. Here is a simple routine you can do on the gym floor or at home.

Why stretch after training

After a hard session your muscles are warm, which makes it the best time to work on flexibility. Slow, held stretches also bring your heart rate down gradually.

Static or dynamic?

Keep dynamic moves, like leg swings and arm circles, for your warm-up. After training, use static stretches: move into position slowly and hold without bouncing.

Stretch to mild tension, never to pain.

A 10-minute cool-down routine

  1. Standing quad stretch, 30 seconds each side.
  2. Seated hamstring stretch, 30 seconds each side.
  3. Hip flexor lunge stretch, 30 seconds each side.
  4. Chest opener against a wall, 30 seconds.
  5. Child's pose, 60 seconds.

Do the floor stretches on a cushioned mat so your knees and hips stay comfortable. Training Mat 12 mm

Common mistakes

  • Holding your breath instead of breathing slowly.
  • Bouncing at the end of the stretch.
  • Rushing through the routine to get out of the gym.
